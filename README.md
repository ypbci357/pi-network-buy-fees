## If you already mined PI: how to deposit and sell

If you've been mining PI on your phone, you can't just send it to an exchange. There are prerequisites:

**1. Complete KYC inside the Pi Network app**

Without Pi Network's own KYC, your tokens stay locked in the testnet and can't be transferred. KYC approval is the gateway to mainnet use.

**2. Migrate to the Open Mainnet**

Inside the Pi app, go to the **Mainnet Checklist** and follow the steps to migrate your balance. Only migrated tokens are transferable. As of mid-2026, Pi Network reported over 16.5 million users had completed KYC and migrated to mainnet.

**3. Check your lockup configuration**

Pi Network lets you voluntarily lock up a percentage of your PI to boost your mining rate. Locked tokens are non-transferable. Before you try to sell, check **Mainnet → Configure Lockup Rate** in the app to see what's actually unlocked. Even unlocked migrated coins are typically locked for 14 days after migration before they become transferable.

**4. Get your OKX PI deposit address**

In the OKX app, go to **Deposit → Deposit crypto → search "PI"**. You'll need to declare the sender (select "My private wallet" if the funds come from your own Pi Wallet). For certain amounts, OKX may require a "Satoshi test" — a small test transaction to verify wallet ownership. Copy the deposit address (it usually starts with "M").

**5. Send PI from your Pi Wallet**

Open the **Pi Browser**, go to **Wallet**, unlock with your passphrase, click **Pay / Request**, manually add your OKX PI deposit address, and send. No memo is required. Wait for blockchain confirmation — your PI will show up in your OKX account.

**6. Sell on the PI/USDT (or PI/USD, PI/EUR, etc.) pair**

PI hit its all-time high of **$3.00 on February 27, 2025**, about a week after open mainnet launch. Since then it's been almost entirely downhill. As of early September 2026, PI trades around **$0.091**, with a market cap of roughly **$1.02 billion** and a circulating supply of about **11.14 billion PI** out of a max supply of 100 billion.

The price action has been driven by a few things:

- **Token unlocks and migration waves** — as more Pioneers complete KYC and migrate, more PI becomes transferable and sellable. Each wave adds selling pressure.

- **No tier-one listings** — without Binance and Coinbase, there's a hard ceiling on inflows. Retail investors who only use those platforms can't buy PI at all.

## The risks you actually need to think about

This is the part most "how to buy PI" guides skim over. A few things to weigh before you put real money in:

**Liquidity risk.** PI's order book is thin. Large orders move the price. If you try to exit a big position in a hurry, you'll eat slippage. Use limit orders, split large trades, and don't assume you can cash out at the last traded price.

**Lockup risk.** If you're buying PI you mined (rather than buying on the open market), your tokens may be locked for years depending on your lockup configuration. Check the Mainnet tab in the Pi app before you assume anything is transferable.

**Counterparty risk.** Leaving PI on an exchange means trusting the exchange. OKX publishes monthly Proof of Reserves showing 1:1 backing of user assets, which is more than most competitors offer, but it's still a centralized exchange. For long-term holdings, move PI to the official Pi Wallet.

**Scam risk.** This is the big one. The landscape around PI is full of fraud:

- **Fake DEX listings** — Pi Core Team has explicitly warned that every PI pair on Uniswap, PancakeSwap, and other DEXs is fraudulent. There is no official DEX market for PI.
- **IOU tokens** — before mainnet launch, some platforms offered "PI IOUs" — speculative contracts that weren't real PI. Most are gone now, but watch for lookalike tokens (PIINU, pPI, etc.).
- **Telegram and Reddit "sellers"** — anyone offering to sell you PI in a DM is almost certainly a scammer. There's no escrow, no chargeback, no recourse.
- **Phishing "wallet unlock" sites** — fake sites that ask for your 24-word passphrase to "unlock" or "migrate" your PI. Real Pi Wallet never asks you to enter your passphrase anywhere except the official Pi Browser app.

If a platform claims you can buy PI without KYC, that's a red flag. Every legitimate exchange that lists mainnet PI requires identity verification.

**Regulatory risk.** PI is not available in all regions on all exchanges. OKX restricts certain products in certain countries. Before you deposit, check that PI trading is actually available in your jurisdiction. Tax treatment also varies — in the US, buying PI with fiat isn't a taxable event, but swapping another crypto for PI is a disposition and may trigger capital gains. Keep records of every trade for tax reporting.
